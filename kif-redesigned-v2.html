Our Mission
Transforming bold ideas into scalable businesses through mentorship, infrastructure, and ecosystem access.

KIPM Innovators Foundation (KIF) is a DPIIT-recognised startup incubator housed at KIPM College of Engineering & Technology, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. Incorporated in September 2022 under the Companies Act, KIF operates as a non-government, not-for-profit entity focused on Community, Personal & Social Services.

We empower early-stage founders across AgTech, AI & Machine Learning, Manufacturing Technologies, Creative Industries, and Environmental Technologies — with the infrastructure, expertise, and community that startups need to thrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

KIF is a DPIIT-recognised startup incubator housed at KIPM College of Engineering & Technology, Gorakhpur. We support early-stage founders with mentorship, infrastructure, funding access, and a strong community network.

You can apply by clicking the "Apply Now" or "WhatsApp Us" button on this page. Our team will reach out to schedule an initial consultation and walk you through the application process.

KIF supports early-stage startups across AgTech, AI & Machine Learning, Manufacturing Technologies, Creative Industries, FinTech, EdTech, and Environmental Technologies.

We welcome mentors, investors, and corporate sponsors. Reach out via WhatsApp or email to explore partnership opportunities — from mentorship sessions to co-investment arrangements.

KIF is a not-for-profit incubator. Many of our core programs, mentorship sessions, and workshops are offered at minimal or no cost to selected startups. Contact us for details on the current cohort.
